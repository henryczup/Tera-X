01 · Introduction

Programmable Cloud Laboratory (PCL) Test Bed: HiveLab

HiveLab is a fully programmable, cloud-accessible laboratory node located in Element Labs at UW–Madison’s University Research Park. It interconnects Tera-X with the UW CHIPS Center, Madison College’s MOSAIC program, UConn, Redwood EDA, Liquid Instruments, Microsanj, Elephas, and Unicorn Biotechnologies. The node demonstrates how automation, AI, and human oversight co-exist to deliver transparent, reproducible science.

Any qualified researcher can sign in, compose a workflow around calibrated instruments, and operate within a digital twin–informed safety envelope. HiveLab prioritizes reproducibility, provenance, and human decision points—automation amplifies scientific judgment instead of replacing it.

02 · Science Drivers

Three intersecting domains anchor HiveLab’s mission.

Semiconductor & Photonic Reliability

Multi-modal stress–measure–relax workflows map reliability landscapes of GaN power devices and photonic structures. Thermal imagers, fast electrical instruments, and digital twins align to capture coupled thermal–electrical behavior.

Biotechnology Microenvironments

Microfluidics, imaging, and autonomous design interrogate cell and organoid responses to chemical, mechanical, and electrical stimuli. Safety-aware AI planners suggest new assays while humans approve every critical step.

Soft Actuators & Compliant Materials

Robotic fixtures, force sensors, and high-speed optics explore nonlinear deformation spaces. Reduced-order twins and learned surrogates adapt as the system uncovers new failure envelopes.

03 · Architecture

Layered Node architecture connecting cloud logic to lab hardware.

Instrumentation Layer

Thermal imagers, fast electrical characterization, optical and fluorescence microscopes, robotic stages, microfluidics, and soft actuator rigs connect through FPGA-enabled adapters that apply sanity checks before orchestration takes control.

Orchestration & Metadata

Workflows are executed as directed graphs of actions and safety checks. Every instrument touch writes structured metadata—calibrations, firmware, samples, and environment—creating replayable experiment logs.

Digital Twins & Analytics

Twins expose prediction APIs, uncertainty estimates, and suggested next steps. They gate AI planners and flag anomalies when behavior departs from validated envelopes.

Human-in-the-Loop Portal

Researchers edit workflows, monitor live data, approve adaptive actions, and review provenance via the cloud portal. Notifications surface anomalies or boundary crossings instantly.

04 · Node Capabilities

Integrated toolsets tuned for autonomy and reproducibility.

Semiconductor & Materials

Microsanj thermal imaging, synchronized electrical test stands, optical diagnostics, and stress cycling routines produce rich datasets for reliability digital twins.

Biotechnology Workflows

Controlled environmental chambers, microfluidic gradient builders, automated dispensers, and live-cell imaging support long horizon, adaptive assays inspired by Elephas and Unicorn Biotechnologies.

Soft Materials & Robotics

Force/displacement sensing, optical tracking, and FPGA-processed signals quantify nonlinear behavior for UConn’s soft actuator studies and broader compliant material discovery.

FPGA-Enhanced Data Pathways

Liquid Instruments and Redwood EDA provide reconfigurable kernels for feature extraction, event detection, and low-latency control, ensuring cloud-scale autonomy without sacrificing fidelity.

05 · AI & Data Standards

Guardrailed AI planning, transparent data lifecycle.

Workflow Planning

Bayesian optimization, reinforcement learners, and gradient-free searches explore parameter spaces. Visual rationales explain every suggestion before it reaches a human approval gate.

Data Integration

Structured schemas unify instrument configs, sample metadata, calibration histories, and digital twin versions—aligned with PCL standards for cross-node reproducibility.

Model Stewardship

Twins are versioned, validated, and refined when deviations appear. AI safety policies prevent self-modifying behavior or unsanctioned instrument control.

Security & Governance

Encrypted comms, isolated control planes, drift detection, and HITL authority ensure no single agent can execute unsafe actions.

06 · Community Integration

Cross-Node Collaboration

Training technicians, students, and professionals on real workflows.

08 · Governance & Access

Transparent policies with humans authorizing every consequential step.

Access Policies

Tiered permissions move users from supervised to advanced modes as they demonstrate proficiency. All actions remain logged for audit.

Human-in-the-Loop Authority

Cloud portal surfaces live data, twin predictions, and safety envelopes; humans approve or halt adaptive actions in real time.

Governance Board

Representatives from Tera-X, UW CHIPS, UConn, Madison College, and partners oversee policies, safety, and performance metrics.
