with Dr. Doug Casa

Saving Lives with Ice and Science

Since suffering from exertional heat stroke during a 10k race 40 years ago, kinesiologist Douglas Casa has dedicated his life and career to maximizing performance and preventing needless tragedy during sport and physical activity. As CEO of the world-renowned Korey Stringer Institute, Casa leads research, outreach, and education efforts to keep people safe the world over.
