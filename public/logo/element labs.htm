WHO IS URP?

University Research Park (URP) provides an atmosphere custom-designed to nurture a productive combination of economic and technological development.

Established in 1984, URP, a University of Wisconsin-Madison affiliate, is an internationally recognized research and technology park that supports early-stage, and growth-oriented businesses in a range of sectors, including engineering, computational and life sciences.

WORLD CLASS FACILITIES

Why URP and Madison, Wisconsin are Models for the Next Great Tech Hub

By 2020, University Research Park’s footprint, the portfolio of labs and offices we manage, will be 40% larger than it was in 2010. From nascent research in lab petri dishes to scaling players investing in new facilities, Madison’s technology cluster is booming.
